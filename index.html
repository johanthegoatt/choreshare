ChoreShare

Who does what this week, split fairly by time, so nobody gets stuck with the bathroom every week.

1 Who lives here?

    Kids or someone away a lot? Set them to a half share and they get about half the time.

    Anyone never home on some days?

    Tick the days and their jobs go to whoever is in.

    Anyone away for a whole week?

    Tick the weeks someone is on holiday or away. They get no jobs that week, and it won't count against their share.

    2 What needs doing?

    ChoreMinutesHow often
      Anyone really hate a chore?

      Tick it and ChoreShare gives it to someone else when that still keeps things fair.

      Anyone who can't do a chore at all?

      For a hard no, like a child and bleach or a bad back. That chore never goes to them unless nobody else can take it.

      3 The rota

      Add at least two people and one chore and your rota shows up here.

      Tap a job to tick it off or swap it to someone else. The rota repeats every 4 weeks.
